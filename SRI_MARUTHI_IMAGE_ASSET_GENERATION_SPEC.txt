SRI MARUTHI ENTERPRISES — IMAGE ASSET GENERATION & MANAGEMENT SPEC

All website imagery is a controlled asset library. Generate/approve assets BEFORE final UI implementation. Cursor must use the attached final assets and must NOT randomly select, replace, or invent images.

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
--------------------
Premium Indian home/lifestyle showroom.
Warm, modern, refined, realistic, trustworthy, aspirational but approachable.

Avoid stock-photo appearance, excessive luxury clichés, futuristic interiors, distorted furniture, clutter, malformed products, watermarks, unwanted text, accidental logos, or unrealistic objects.

HERO — hero-home.webp
---------------------
Designed specifically for the right side of the homepage hero.

Composition:
LEFT = visually quiet/darker negative space for navy gradient and text.
RIGHT/CENTER-RIGHT = strong living-room/furniture subject.

The image must support a LEFT-side gradient fade while keeping the RIGHT photographic area clear.

Do not embed headline, buttons, navigation or logo in the image.

Recommended working size: 1920×1080 or larger.

-----------------------
Five individual assets:
furniture-3d.png

STRICTLY 3D.

Do not use photographs, stock images, emojis, flat SVG icons, outline icons, cartoon icons or unrelated illustrations.

All five must share:
- same camera angle
- same perspective
- same scale
- same lighting direction
- same studio lighting
- same material realism
- same shadow softness
- same visual density
- same rendering quality
